My Approach

Three steps,
one goal.

Every project starts with a clear problem and an honest assessment of what the technology can actually do.

  1. 1

    Listen & scope

    Understand what the user actually needs. What exists, what's missing, and which constraints matter most.

  2. 2

    Build & verify

    Write clean, readable code. Prefer the simplest solution that solves the problem. Test as you go, not after the deadline.

  3. 3

    Ship & iterate

    Deploy early, get feedback, and refine. I'd rather have a small, correct product than a big, unfinished one.
