Document intelligence · 2026

hamiGenZ

hamiGenZ helps people in Nepal make complex public-facing documents easier to understand. I built a multilingual, source-grounded workflow combining document processing, retrieval, and cited answers in Nepali, Romanized Nepali, and English.

Public demo: The online version uses predefined sample responses to show document analysis and citations. Live AI processing requires a configured local backend.

Sports simulation · 2026

Arena_Core

Arena_Core brings competition data, tournament draws, and match simulation into one browser-based platform. I implemented interactive group and knockout stages, standings, and deterministic match rules—including a 1.15 home-advantage multiplier and a 78% penalty-conversion probability.
